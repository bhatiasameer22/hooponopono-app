Privacy Policy

App: Ho'oponopono Healing & Meditation
Last updated: October 2026

1. What data we collect

We collect no personal data. The app does not require you to create an account, log in, or share any information with us.

Everything you enter in the app — your name, journal entries, progress, favourites and reminder settings — is stored only on your device using local storage. It never leaves your phone.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • No email address, phone number or name
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or crash reports sent to any server
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No cookies or tracking

3. Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications — to send you optional daily reminders you set yourself. You can turn these off at any time in the app or in your phone settings.
  • Foreground service / wake lock — to keep audio playing when your screen is off. No data is collected through this.

4. Third-party services

The app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising or data platforms. There are no ads in the app.

5. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal data, no special handling of children's data is required.

6. Data deletion

To erase all your data, open the app → More → Reset all data. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data from your device.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how data is handled (for example, when adding cloud features), we will update this page and notify you within the app.
