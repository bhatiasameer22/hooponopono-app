Find peace, release what no longer serves you, and heal from the inside out — with the ancient Hawaiian practice of Ho'oponopono.

Ho'oponopono (ho-oh-pono-pono) is a simple but profound practice of forgiveness and reconciliation. It uses four healing phrases — I'm sorry. Please forgive me. Thank you. I love you. — to clear painful memories, difficult relationships, and emotional blocks.

This app brings the full practice to your pocket.

✦ GUIDED HEALING SCRIPTS
Choose from 10 deep healing scripts — for relationships, money, anger, anxiety, self-love, health, inner child healing, habits, feeling stuck, and more. Each script walks you through the four Ho'oponopono phrases in a personal, step-by-step way.

✦ FIND YOUR SCRIPT
Describe what's troubling you — type "fight with my wife", "money stress", "feeling worthless" — and the app matches you with the right script instantly.

✦ MEDITATIONS & MANTRAS
Peaceful guided meditations paired with healing frequencies (396 Hz, 528 Hz, 639 Hz). Includes the Ho'oponopono Mantra, I Choose Peace, Return to Peace, and more. Audio continues playing with your screen off.

✦ DAILY HEALING FEED
Swipe through 277 healing affirmations and meditation lines — a new way to start your day.

✦ MORNING AFFIRMATIONS
Begin each morning with a personalised affirmation carousel for self-love, forgiveness, peace and abundance.

✦ 21-DAY CHALLENGE
Build a lasting Ho'oponopono habit with a structured 21-day programme. Track your progress every day.

✦ 55×5 WRITING PRACTICE
